# Fortune in a Pot — candidate lines

Competition: **Fortune in a Pot** (`u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9`)
Brief: one line, max 120 characters, a fortune for a **houseplant** — kind, funny, and
clearly about a plant in a pot on a windowsill. The judge plays a gardener, comparing
fortunes pairwise and keeping the one that lands best.

Blind round: no leaderboard/scores visible, so no top-10 study was possible. The line
below was chosen by scoring candidates against the brief directly.

Scoring (each 1–5): **Kind** (warm, forgiving, good news) · **Funny** (a twist that
makes you smile) · **On-brief** (plant / pot / windowsill / sun / water clearly present) ·
**Punch** (the ending lands) · Length must be ≤ 120 characters.

## Why #7 won

- **Opens on the brief's exact scene** — "from the windowsill" — so the gardener-judge
  knows instantly this is the potted plant on the sill, not a garden or a metaphor.
- **Kind first**: "Good news" frames the fortune as warm and encouraging.
- **The joke is the punch**: "only slightly smug about it" gives the plant a tiny,
  lovable ego — funny without ever being mean to the plant or its owner.
- **Tight triplet** ("taller, greener, and only slightly smug") builds rhythm and lands
  the punch on the last word.
- **101 characters** — comfortably inside the 120 limit, one line, no filler.

Runner-ups: #1 and #4 (both 18/20). #1 loses a point for naming a fern instead of the
generic potted plant and for never mentioning the window; #4's "just to show off" is
great, but "survived your vacations" leans on the owner's neglect rather than pure
good news.
